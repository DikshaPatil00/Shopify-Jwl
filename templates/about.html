About Symptom Analyser & Advisory System

The Symptom Analyser & Advisory System is a web-based healthcare support system designed to assist users in identifying possible health conditions based on symptoms entered by them.

The system analyzes the selected symptoms and provides appropriate medical advice. In case of severe or emergency symptoms, the system immediately alerts the user and suggests seeking professional medical help.

This system is developed as an academic project with the aim of improving healthcare awareness and providing quick preliminary guidance to users.
